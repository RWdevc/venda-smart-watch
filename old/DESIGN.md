## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-conversion, luxury-tech sales environment tailored for next-generation smart wearable devices. The brand persona blends the precision horology of luxury Swiss watchmakers with the cutting-edge digital craftsmanship seen in Apple and Garmin flagship releases.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Sleek & Stealth:** Dominated by absolute black, tactile obsidian surfaces, and clean monochromatic framing.
- **Biometric Vitality:** Vibrant biological accents—deep violet and high-voltage neon green—communicate peak athletic readiness and responsive biometric feedback, while surgical accents signify cardiovascular monitoring and critical real-time alerts.
- **High-Scarcity & High-Value:** Clean monochrome contrasts create urgency and exclusivity around pricing, guarantee seals, and limited-run offers.
- **Audience:** Tech-forward professionals, fitness enthusiasts, and gadget collectors seeking high-end build aesthetics (such as stainless-steel milanese mesh bands and dynamic bezel watch faces) paired with medical-grade health monitoring and seamless mobile connectivity.

### Design Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **Dark-Mode Glassmorphism** with **Precision High-Contrast Editorial E-commerce**. Key characteristics include:
- Multi-layered frosted glass panels (`backdrop-blur-xl`) with ultra-fine, hairline luminous borders.
- Sub-surface radial lighting effects that isolate the matte-black metal casing and high-resolution AMOLED displays of the product.
- High-visibility conversion anchors that pulse with kinetic ambient glow to steer buyer attention decisively through the checkout funnel.

## Colors

The chromatic structure is built upon an ultra-deep dark palette optimized for OLED and high-contrast digital displays, giving maximum luminescence to product photography and biometric data points.

### Primary (`#000000` - Absolute Black)
The foundational contrast and structural hue. Used for core canvas depth, framing, and clean monochromatic text elements.

### Secondary (`#8018b0` - Royal Violet)
The connectivity and smart interface accent. Reflects Bluetooth data transfer, voice AI visualizers, weather tracking, and active sync status badges.

### Tertiary (`#1CFF00` - Acid Green)
The biometric sensor and urgency anchor. Emulates continuous optical heart rate sensors, SpO2 alerts, countdown timer stress points, and call disconnect interactions.

### Neutral Architecture (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White Canvas)
- **Canvas Base:** `#08090C` (Absolute deep neutral background).
- **Surface Layer 1 (Cards & Modules):** `#111318` at 70% opacity with subtle ambient noise.
- **Surface Layer 2 (Elevated Sheets & Floating Banners):** `#181B22` with frosted sheen.
- **Border / Structural Lines:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to maintain architectural boundaries without competing with watch hardware visuals.
- **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Ultra-crisp white for maximum legibility).
- **Text Secondary:** `#8E95A5` (Graphite silver for technical specifications and non-critical subtitles).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is orchestrated to combine high-impact retail presence with technical precision:

- **Display & Headlines (`Outfit`):** Geometric, modern, and commanding. With tightened letter-spacing and substantial weights (700/800), it delivers strong marketing copy, luxury feature callouts, and striking product titles.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, open letterforms that remain highly readable against dark backgrounds. Used for value propositions, medical sensor disclaimers, and warranty terms.
- **Data & Micro-Labels (`Space Grotesk`):** A technical monospace-inflected sans serif reserved for telemetry indicators (BPM counters, Bluetooth versioning, step trackers, countdown timers, price tags, and inventory indicators).

### Responsive Reflow
On desktop, `display-hero` commands the view at `56px` alongside massive product renders. On mobile viewports under 640px, typography automatically shifts down to `display-hero-mobile` (`36px`) and `headline-xl-mobile` (`28px`), preventing line wrapping of high-converting marketing hooks.

## Layout & Spacing

The sales layout adheres to an intentional rhythm designed to guide scrolling momentum and eliminate buyer fatigue:

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column fluid grid locked to a max width of `1280px`, with `gutter-lg` (`2rem`) and `margin-desktop` (`3rem`). Used for dual-column arrangements.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `gutter` (`1.5rem`) and outer margins of `2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column dynamic grid with `gutter-sm` (`0.75rem`) and `margin-mobile` (`1rem`).

### Spacing Scale
- `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-text spacing, rating star gaps, badge internal padding.
- `space-md` (16px) to `space-lg` (24px): Card interior paddings, input field heights, form grouping gutters.
- `space-xl` (40px) to `space-3xl` (96px): Section transitions, distinguishing feature breakdowns.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy on dark surfaces relies on layered luminescence and glassmorphism rather than muddy shadows:

### Layering Model
1. **Base Matrix (Elevation 0):** `#08090C` background, textured with low-intensity violet and acid green radial gradient orbs positioned behind floating watch hardware.
2. **Glassmorphic Paneling (Elevation 1):** Feature cards, comparison grids, and spec breakdown modules utilize a semi-translucent fill of `rgba(17, 19, 24, 0.65)` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
3. **Floating Directives (Elevation 2):** Floating sticky checkout bars, active dropdowns, and modal dialogs adopt `rgba(24, 27, 34, 0.90)` with an ambient shadow.
4. **Kinetic Glow (Conversion Accentuation):** Primary CTA buttons and key biometric metrics feature an ambient glow ring utilizing the theme's high-contrast accents (`#1CFF00` and `#8018b0`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry that directly echoes the curved metallic bezels, circular glass lenses, and ergonomic silicone/milanese bands of the smartwatch hardware:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to badges, numeric counters, input fields, and compact feature chips.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / 16px):** Applied to technical cards, testimonial blocks, watch face thumbnail selectors, and floating modular panels.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Applied to major checkout highlight cards, guarantee hero banners, and offer bundles.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for high-conversion action triggers and live biometric floating capsules.

## Components

### High-Conversion Buy Buttons
- **Primary Pulsing CTA:** Styled with a striking gradient from `#000000` to `#8018b0`, accented by `#1CFF00`. Features bold uppercase text (`Outfit`, 600 weight), a full pill radius, and a continuous breathing glow.
- **Secondary / Installment Button:** Outlined transparent button (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`) with a light glass surface on hover and sharp white text.
- **Interactive State:** `transform: translateY(-2px)` on hover, with a rapid scale down (`0.98`) on tap for immediate tactile feedback.

### Glassmorphic Feature Cards
- Constructed with `background: rgba(17, 19, 24, 0.65)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, and rounded corners of `1rem`.
- Features an ultra-fine perimeter line (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Displays watch capability icons illuminated with violet or acid green gradients.

### Watch Face & Strap Selectors (Chips)
- Circular and pill-shaped radio chips displaying material previews.
- **Selected State:** Glowing accent halo border (`2px solid #1CFF00`) with a check indicator badge.
- **Unselected State:** Matte graphite surface (`#181B22`) with low-contrast borders.

### Conversion Badges & Scarcity Elements
- **Offer Badges:** Pill-shaped capsules with high-contrast monochrome and acid green highlights conveying immediate scarcity.
- **Live Social Proof Popups:** Small floating glassmorphism cards at the bottom corner showing real-time purchases, pairing a verified tick in `#1CFF00` with a product thumbnail.

### Biometric & Technical Telemetry Indicators
- Horizontal progress bars and circular rings displaying vitals.
- Background tracks use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, filled with neon gradients (`#1CFF00` for active metrics, `#8018b0` for sync states).

### Form Inputs & One-Click Checkout Fields
- Dark field surface (`#111318`) with interior shadow and rounded corners of `0.5rem`.
- Focus state activates an intense kinetic border glow (`border-color: #8018b0`, `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(128, 24, 176, 0.15)`).
- Labels styled in `Plus Jakarta Sans` at 13px with silver contrast (`#8E95A5`).
